Common Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

How is a "Document" defined?

A document can be a PDF, Word file, text file, or a unique website URL synchronized to your knowledge base.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption and guarantee your private business data is never used to train foundational AI models.

Do you offer local currency billing?

Yes, we support billing in INR for our customers in India, as well as multiple other global currencies.
