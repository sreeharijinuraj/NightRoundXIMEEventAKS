Common Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and we pro-rate any differences.

How is a "Knowledge Source" defined?

A knowledge source can be a PDF, a Word document, a website URL, or a database connection. Each unique file or link counts as one source.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption and do not use your private business data to train our foundational models.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Yes, choosing annual billing gives you 2 months free (approx. 17% discount) on any of our paid plans.
